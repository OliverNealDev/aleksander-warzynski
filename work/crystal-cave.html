Stylized Crystal Cave

A modular environment built as a tool for level designers. It was my Bachelor research project into modular modelling for games, and it earned a high distinction of 82%.

The scene

Exterior

Giant crystals are the cave’s light source. Warm gold spills across the rock and the arched buildings, set against cool blue fog in the depths. The buildings are assembled from the modular kit.

The scene

Interiors

Research

The modular kit

The research side of the project focused on good modular practice and how to structure a kit as a tool for level designers, so a small number of pieces can build a lot of environment.

The architecture kit is 13 pieces and 4,070 triangles: walls, a window wall, an arch with a vaulted ceiling, pillars, a round platform, and props such as the lamp post, table and stool. Alongside it sit the cave pieces: boulders, rock pillars, basalt-style columns, stalactites and crystals.

Materials

Trim sheet & tileables

This project was also where I tackled the areas of asset creation I was weakest in: Substance 3D Designer and trim sheets. It took a lot of experimenting to get right.

The buildings share one trim sheet baked in Substance 3D Painter. Marble panels, wooden beams, gold moulding and a carved frieze all live on it, and the wall, window and arch pieces are UV-mapped onto its strips. The stone floor is a tileable material built in Designer and finished in Painter.

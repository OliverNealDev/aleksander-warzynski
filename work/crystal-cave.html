Stylized Crystal Cave

Modular environment, Bachelor final project, 2026

This was my big Bachelor project, and I focused my research on modular modelling in games. I spent a lot of time learning good modular practice and how to structure a kit so it works as a tool for level designers.

I also used it to work on the areas I was weakest in, mainly Substance 3D Designer and trim sheets. It took a while to figure things out and apply what I’d researched, but I finished with a high distinction of 82% for the module.

Role
Solo project
Course
BSc (Hons) Games, Newcastle College University Centre
Grade
82%, high distinction
Kit
13 architecture pieces, 4,070 triangles
Software
Unreal Engine, Blender, Substance 3D Designer, Substance 3D Painter, GIMP

The cave

The giant crystals are the cave’s main light source. Warm gold spills across the rock and the buildings, against cool blue fog down in the depths. Every building in the scene is put together from the modular kit.

Interiors

The kit

The idea was that a small number of pieces could build a lot of environment. The architecture kit is 13 pieces and 4,070 triangles in total: walls, a window wall, an arch with a vaulted ceiling, pillars, a round platform, and props like the lamp post, table and stool. Alongside it are the cave pieces, which are boulders, rock pillars, basalt-style columns, stalactites and the crystals.

Trim sheet and materials

The buildings all share one trim sheet, baked in Substance 3D Painter. Marble panels, wooden beams, gold moulding and a carved frieze are all on it, and the wall, window and arch pieces are UV mapped onto its strips. The stone floor is a tileable material I built in Substance 3D Designer and finished in Painter.

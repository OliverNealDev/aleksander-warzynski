Ritual Prop Set

Three game-ready props, BSc (Hons) Games coursework, 2025

Three game-ready assets I made for my BSc (Hons) Games course: a sacrificial dagger, a ritual table and a cultist tent. I designed them as a set for a cult that worships a goat-like deity, so they share the same weathered bronze bells, curled horns and deep red cloth.

Each one has a full breakdown. Use the buttons under the images to switch between the final render, each texture map, a clay render and the wireframe.

Role
Solo, from design to texturing
Course
BSc (Hons) Games, Newcastle College University Centre
Software
Blender, Substance 3D Painter, GIMP, Krita, PureRef

Sacrificial Dagger

A detailed stylised dagger whose design shows off its ritual purpose. Details like the bell and the eye hint at the goat-like deity behind the cult, and the glyphs on the blade are baked into the normal map.

Ritual Table

An altar with a horned stone backboard, a bell hanging in its window, a draped cloth, a skull and cloven-hoofed legs. The bell and horns carry the dagger’s motifs over to a bigger piece of set dressing.

Ritual Table on ArtStation

Cultist Tent

A striped canvas tent pinned down with wooden pegs and bound with rope, with the set’s bronze bell over the entrance and a patterned rug inside. The cloth folds are carried in the normal map over clean, even topology.

Ritual Prop Set

Three game-ready props for a cult devoted to a goat-like deity: a sacrificial dagger, a ritual table and a cultist tent. They were designed as one set, sharing weathered bronze bells, curling horns and deep red cloth so they read as belonging to the same world.

Role
Solo: design, modelling, baking, texturing
Context
BSc (Hons) Games coursework, 2025
Dagger
5,896 tris
Tools
Blender, Substance 3D Painter, GIMP, Krita, PureRef

Use the buttons under each prop to flip between the final render and its individual maps.

5,896 tris
Prop 01

Sacrificial Dagger

A detailed stylised dagger whose design conveys its ritual purpose. Key details like the bell and the eye hint at the goat-like deity behind the cult. Engraved glyphs on the blade are baked into the normal map.

  • 5,896 tris
  • Blender
  • Substance 3D Painter
  • Krita

Prop 02

Ritual Table

An altar with a horned stone backboard, a bell hung in its window, a draped cloth, a skull and cloven-hoofed legs. The bell and horns carry the dagger’s motifs over to a larger piece of set dressing.

  • Blender
  • Substance 3D Painter

Prop 03

Cultist Tent

A striped canvas tent pinned with wooden pegs and bound with rope, with the set’s bronze bell hanging over the entrance and a patterned rug inside. Cloth folds are carried in the normal map over clean, even topology.

Shri Devi’s Dice

Offhand weapon for INFINITY27’s Samsara, 2026

I made this offhand item during INFINITY27’s four-week training bootcamp, for their game Samsara. The brief was the divination dice used by the goddess Shri Devi, who is also known as Palden Lhamo.

After I finished the bootcamp and got my certificate, INFINITY27 offered me a contract to keep working on it. That gave me time to polish the parts that needed it, mainly the texturing and the in-game physics, so it was ready for the shipping build.

Role
Research, modelling, texturing, engine setup

In game

Here it is in Samsara. The dice hang from the weapon on cords and have physics, so they swing around as the character moves. Michael Humphriss did the programming side of the item.

Research and design

I started by researching Tibetan divination dice, thangka paintings of Palden Lhamo and the lore behind her dice. My Miro boards also covered materials (metal, gems, wood and yak bone), a colour palette and the game’s own art style.

From there I sketched ten silhouettes and mocked up how each one would sit on the character in game. The final design was number 10, which takes its flame crest and curved bar straight from the thangka reference.

Modelling

I blocked it out in mid-poly first, then sculpted the high-poly detail for the crest, bar, handle and dice. The low-poly came in at 15,552 triangles, well under the budget of around 25k. Feedback during the bootcamp asked for some light wear, which I carried through into the texturing.

Texturing

Everything is textured in Substance 3D Painter on one UV set: worn bronze, lapis-blue dice with engraved symbols, a wooden handle and the fiery crest. It exports as three 4K maps using Unreal’s standard AORM packing, and the engine downscales them as needed.

What I took from it

This project helped me get to grips with the industry-standard workflow for games: working to a studio’s brief and art style, taking regular feedback, and using Perforce version control with Unreal Engine and Hansoft for tasks. Coming back to it under contract also showed me what it takes to get an asset ready to ship.

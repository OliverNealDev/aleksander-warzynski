Character Rig & Animation Set

A full character rig built in Maya, driving eight game animations set up in Unreal Engine. Maya wasn’t my comfort zone at first, but I pulled through and scored 88% for the module.

Animation showcase in Unreal Engine (1 min, with sound).

Animation

Eight animations

Each animation came with its own challenges and quirks, and I worked to keep them cohesive and consistent in quality. Two were built specifically for additive blending with the other animations inside the engine.

  • Idle
  • Walk
  • Run
  • Look around
  • Stretch
  • Wave
  • Dance
  • Death
Rigging

Built to be animated

I built the rig with accurate skin weights and convenient controls, so it would be quick to animate with later.

  • IK/FK armsA switch control on each arm blends the upper arm, lower arm and wrist between IK and FK chains.
  • Finger controlsPinky, ring, middle, index and thumb curls live as attributes on the arm switch, so hands pose from one control.
  • Head space switchingThe FK head control can follow the chest or hold its orientation in world space.
  • Colour-coded controlsLeft and right sides are colour-coded, with IK foot controls, knee pole vectors and torso rings for readability in the viewport.

The character model is a Lethal Company model from Sketchfab and was not made by me. The rig, skinning and animation are my own work.

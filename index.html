About

I’m Aleksander, but you’ll find me online as FLOART. I’ve been studying games at Newcastle College since 2021, starting with a BTEC and finishing with my BSc this year.

Hard-surface modelling is where I’m happiest. I like how organised and structured the process is, but I’m always up for characters when a project needs them. Outside of my degree I’ve made game jam and passion projects, including a Norse mythology FPS where I made most of the props, environments and character models.

I do my best work in a team, bouncing ideas and feedback around until a piece is as good as it can be. I want to keep getting better as an artist and work on games that try something new. VR in particular still feels like it has a lot of untapped potential.

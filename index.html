About

About Aleksander

I’m Aleksander, also known as FLOART: a 3D artist from the North East of England who makes props and environments for games.

Hard-surface modelling is where I’m happiest. The organised, structured process just clicks with me, though I’m always up for characters when a project needs them. Outside my degree I’ve made game-jam and passion projects, including a Norse-mythology FPS where I handled most of the props, environments and character models.

I do my best work in a team, trading ideas and feedback to get a piece to its strongest version. I want to keep improving as an artist and bring those skills to games that try something new. VR in particular still feels like a space with a lot of untapped potential.
